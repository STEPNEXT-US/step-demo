FAQ

Common questions.

Is there a free trial?
We don't offer a traditional time-limited trial. Instead, we give you 2 months of Core for free when you sign up with a referral code from another dojang. Plus, every plan comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
How does the referral program work?
When a STEP Partner refers your dojang, both of you get 2 months of Core for free. As you refer more dojangs, you climb the STEP Partner ranks — earning STEP Coin Credits, business trip rewards, and VIP experiences.
Should I start with Core or Pro?
Most established dojangs start with Pro — it includes parent-facing tools, tuition automation, and the full Xiro AI experience. Core is ideal if you're just digitizing operations and don't yet need parent connection or auto-billing.
What's beyond Pro? When can I access more?
Once your dojang is running smoothly on Pro, deeper capabilities unlock — including AI automation, marketing tools, and a website builder. We invite Pro masters to the next stage when their operation is ready, typically after a few months of consistent use. No need to choose now.
Can I switch plans anytime?
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. When upgrading, the new features become available immediately and we prorate the difference. When downgrading, the change takes effect at the next billing cycle.
How do payments work for my students?
The Pro plan includes Stripe Connect integration. Parents pay directly through the Play STEP app — you never handle their card details. Standard Stripe processing fees apply (2.9% + 30¢) plus a small STEP platform fee.
What if I have multiple dojang locations?
Each location is billed separately. You can manage multiple dojangs from a single account, switching between them in the header. Volume discounts available for 5+ locations — contact us.
Is my data safe? Can I export it?
Your data is yours. You can export your full member roster, attendance records, and financial data at any time. We're SOC 2 compliant and GDPR/CCPA ready. We never sell your data — ever.
